## IAMJARL

I'm Jarl, a designer who builds things. Based in Copenhagen.

I work alone and I do the whole thing. The idea, the interface, the code, the icon, the
marketing site, the store copy and the screenshots. The taste and the mistakes are both mine.

The range is the part I care about. Metal shaders and CoreAudio at one end, generative WebGL
and synthesis at the other, and a shared design system holding it together so that an app, its
website and its store screenshots look like they came from the same hand.

### The system underneath

[iamjarl-design](https://github.com/JarlLyng/iamjarl-design) is design tokens and components
shared across three runtimes: the web, SwiftUI and React Native. One definition of a color, a
radius or a shadow, compiled out to CSS custom properties, a Swift package and an npm package.
It is the reason a portfolio built by one person does not look like a portfolio built by eleven.

[nostromo-ui](https://github.com/JarlLyng/nostromo-ui) is a React component library built
alongside it. 30 components, 6 marketing blocks, 4 themes, WCAG 2.1 AA.

### How I think about it

Native, minimal, private by default. The work happens on the device, nothing needs an account,
nothing tracks anyone. I would rather say what a thing leaves out than pad a feature list, and
I would rather finish something small than nearly finish something large.
