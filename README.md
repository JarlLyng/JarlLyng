## Jarl

Designer who builds things. Copenhagen.

Fifteen years in digital design and development, and I still do both rather than pick one.
Some weeks that means typography and interface, other weeks a Metal shader, a Swift package
or a build pipeline. What I like is the whole arc. The idea, the craft, and the unglamorous
last mile that turns it into something a stranger can actually use.

This account is a slice of it, the part that happens to be code.

**IAMJARL** is the name I ship my own work under. Small native Apple apps and web tools, held
together by a design system that spans the web, SwiftUI and React Native, so one person's
output looks like one person's output. [iamjarl.com](https://iamjarl.com) has all of it.
Some of the repositories here are open source.

Mostly Swift and SwiftUI, TypeScript, Three.js and Metal.
